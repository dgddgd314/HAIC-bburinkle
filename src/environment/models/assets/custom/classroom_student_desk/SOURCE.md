# Source

This classroom desk reuses the geometry and collision proportions of the
installed THOR `Desk_311_1` asset. Its original dark material is replaced by
the installed THOR `TexturesCom_WoodFine0050_1_seamless_S_pale.png` texture so
the shared scene has a portable, light-oak classroom variant.

Dimensions are approximately 1.77 × 0.84 × 0.80 m after the editor's base
orientation is applied.
